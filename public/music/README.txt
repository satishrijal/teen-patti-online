Background music for Teen Patti Online
======================================
Drop your MP3 file in THIS folder and name it exactly:

    background.mp3

The game will then play it on loop (at low volume) once the player
first taps/clicks anywhere. A music button in the top bar mutes/unmutes
it, and the choice is remembered.

Ideas: a Dashain/festival song you have the rights to, or royalty-free
Nepali folk music.

IMPORTANT: only use music you have the rights to use. Don't upload
copyrighted songs you don't own or have a license for.

No file here = no music, and the game works exactly the same.
